Plan an Iowa City Wedding Without Guessing at Prices

Prairie Vow Guide was started by Marisol Tran, a former event coordinator, and Dev Okafor, a budget analyst who got married on a tight budget himself. We collect venue fees, vendor rates, and rental costs for the Iowa City and Coralville area in one place, so couples can compare options before they book a single tour.

Every price on this site is a typical starting figure for a Saturday in the May to October season. Weekday and off-season dates are often 15 to 30 percent lower, so always ask.

Three Tips From Our Team

Book a Friday or Sunday. Many Iowa City venues discount non-Saturday dates, and vendors are easier to schedule.

Ask what is included. A venue that includes tables, chairs, and a setup crew can cost less than a cheaper space that needs separate rentals.

Plan for football weekends. Home game days fill hotels and slow traffic across town, so tell guests early and consider avoiding those dates.
