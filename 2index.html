Affordable Wedding Venues in Iowa City

A wedding does not need a $6,000 ballroom to feel special. The five fictional venues below all charge $2,500 or less for the rental, and each one has a way to stretch your budget further. Prices are typical Saturday starting fees.

Our Picks

Willow Bend Park Shelter

Up to 90 guests | $350 for 6 hours | Outdoor, covered

A city-style park shelter beside a slow creek, with picnic tables, grills, and a paved path for a ceremony walk. It is the lowest-cost option on our list. You bring everything else, so budget for a tent or backup plan in case of rain.

The Old Depot Hall

Up to 120 guests | $1,400 for 8 hours | Indoor, historic

A restored 1920s railway depot with exposed brick, tall windows, and a small kitchen for caterers. The fee includes 12 round tables and 120 chairs, which saves about $600 in rentals.

Maple Ridge Grange

Up to 150 guests | $1,100 for 10 hours | Indoor, rural

A country community hall ten minutes outside town with a large dance floor and free parking. Outside catering and alcohol are allowed, which keeps food costs flexible. The hall is not air conditioned, so it works best for spring and fall dates.

Brightside Community Center

Up to 100 guests | $900 for 6 hours | Indoor, modern

A bright multipurpose room with a built-in sound system and a projector for slideshows. Friday evening rates drop to $650. Decorations must be removable, and open flames are not permitted.

Cedar Lane Barn Loft

Up to 80 guests | $2,300 for 8 hours | Indoor and outdoor

A small family farm with a renovated hayloft and a lawn for the ceremony. It sits at the top of our budget range, but it includes a coordinator for the day and a rain plan in the main barn.

How to Save Even More

Choose a weekday or Sunday. Four of the five venues above reduce their fee by 20 to 35 percent for these dates.

Skip the second venue. Holding the ceremony and reception in one place can save $500 or more in rentals and transportation.

Watch the hidden fees. Ask about cleaning charges, security deposits, insurance requirements, and overtime rates before you sign.

Use what is already there. Historic halls and barns need little decoration, so you can spend less on florals and signs.

Questions to Ask on a Tour

What is the total cost with tax and fees? How many hours do we get for setup and cleanup? Is there a rain plan? Can we hire our own caterer and bring our own drinks? What is the refund policy if we need to change the date?
