Small Wedding Venues in Iowa City

More couples are choosing guest lists of 60 or fewer. A smaller wedding lets you spend more per guest on food, flowers, and photography, and it makes room for real conversations with everyone who came. These five fictional venues are built for intimate celebrations.

About Each Venue

Quill & Ivy Library Room

A private reading room above a downtown bookshop, with floor-to-ceiling shelves and warm lamp lighting. Ceremonies fit easily, and a small catered dinner can be served at two long tables. Music must stay quiet, so it suits couples who want a calm evening.

Glasshouse Garden Studio

A working greenhouse with ferns, citrus trees, and a gravel aisle. The plants do most of the decorating, so most couples spend little on florals. It is warm in summer, so morning and early evening ceremonies are best.

Riverbank Porch Inn

A six-room bed-and-breakfast with a wraparound porch and a view of the river. Your fee includes two nights in the bridal suite, which makes it a popular pick for elopement-style weekends with a few close family members.

The Walnut Table Loft

A private upstairs room in a farm-to-table restaurant. The kitchen handles food and drinks, so there is no need to hire a caterer. Menu packages run $48 to $75 per person on top of the room fee.

Hillcrest Chapel Garden

A small stone chapel with a flowering garden next door. It seats 60 for a ceremony and offers a covered patio for a cocktail hour. Photographers love the late afternoon light across the lawn.

Planning a Small Wedding

Spend where guests notice. With 30 guests, upgrading the menu from $40 to $75 per person adds only $1,050 to your total.

Check your layout. A room that feels cozy for 25 can feel empty with 8, so ask the venue for a floor plan that matches your headcount.

Confirm rules for small spaces. Some venues limit amplified music, candles, or confetti. Ask before you plan your decor.
